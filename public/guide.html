How to play

Choose a numbered Across or Down clue. Type its answer and press Claim. Each answer can be claimed once; its letters become visible in your colour and help everyone solve crossing clues.

Try a clue

1 Across: Fruit used in cider (5)

Earn power

Answers have 4–10 letters. Base power is length minus 3. Consecutive claims double your multiplier: 1×, 2×, 4×, 8×. Another player's claim breaks your streak; a new grid does not.

Attack or Guard

Attack an opponent for your earned power, or Guard to restore health and convert excess to shields, capped at starting health. Shields absorb damage first.

Next puzzle

All clues solved or all surviving players vote to skip: reveal the solution and load a fresh crossword. Last player or team standing wins. Host can play again; lobby points carry over.
